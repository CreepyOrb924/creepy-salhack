## Known remaining fidelity work

- StashFinder historical flight path, complete original HUD/components/font behavior, full command syntax and several existing reduced modules/settings still need implementation/review.
- Crystal variants currently share a modern engine; registering the three names does not reproduce all original algorithms or settings.
- Builder/Printer support has explicit shape, file-format and blockstate limitations.
- Modern chunk, entity ownership, packet exploit and rendering behavior differs from 1.12; group notes document those limits.
- Targeted camera, inventory, combat and combined-client checks do not cover every enabled mode or remote-server acceptance. See PORT3-TESTING.md for the exact tested scope.
- Preserve explicit exclusions for crashers, malicious behavior, illegal-item and obsolete duplication exploits. Discord RPC is not implemented.
